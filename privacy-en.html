Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the mobile game Asteroid Hopper (the "Game"), developed by Gamesada, uses and how. The Game has no accounts or sign-in; we do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

1. Information that stays on your device

  • Game progress: scores, crystals, purchased/selected looks and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Photo Pilot: if you use this feature, the photo you choose is cropped and resized on your device and stored only there. It is never uploaded to any server or shared. You pick it through the phone's own file picker; the Game does not request gallery or camera permission. You can delete it at any time with "Remove photo".

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This information is processed directly by Google; we do not access it.

  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see Google's consent form on first launch. You can accept or refuse personalised ads and change your choice later under "Ad privacy settings" in the Game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • More: How Google uses data from partner sites/apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Your payment details never reach us. The Game only receives which product was bought, to deliver it and to support "Restore purchases".

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Deleting data

Uninstalling the Game deletes all game data on the device, including progress and the photo. We hold no data linked to you. For Google's advertising data, use Google's own tools.

6. Changes and contact
